# Vendor SLA Reference

## GPU Hardware Vendors

**Vendor A (Primary GPU supplier)**
- RMA turnaround: 5 business days standard, 24 hours for expedited (SEV-1 only, requires VP approval)
- Advance replacement available for accounts above $2M annual spend
- Escalation contact: only via Vendor Relations, not direct engineering contact

**Vendor B (Secondary GPU supplier)**
- RMA turnaround: 10 business days standard
- No advance replacement program
- Used as overflow capacity only; do not route SEV-1 hardware issues here first

## Power & Facilities Vendors

- Generator maintenance contractor: 4-hour response SLA for facilities SEV-1
- Grid interconnect utility: no formal SLA; treat all utility-caused outages as outside our control for postmortem purposes, but still document customer impact

## Logistics & Freight

- Standard freight: 7-10 day transit for GPU pallets, site to site
- Expedited air freight: 24-48 hours, requires Manufacturing & Supply Chain lead sign-off due to cost (~8x standard rate)
- Customs delays are the #1 cause of missed site go-live dates; flag any international shipment 30+ days before target site power-on

## Renewal & Contract Notes

- All vendor contracts with auto-renewal clauses must be reviewed by Legal at least 60 days before renewal date
- Vendor performance scorecards are due quarterly to Vendor Relations
