# Procurement & Spend Approval Matrix

## Approval Thresholds

| Spend Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under $10K | Team lead |
| $10K - $100K | Department director |
| $100K - $1M | VP of function + Finance review |
| Over $1M | CFO + CEO sign-off |

## Fast-Track Exceptions

- Emergency hardware replacement tied to an open SEV-1 incident may bypass the standard approval queue with a single VP approval, regardless of amount, but must be logged retroactively in the procurement system within 24 hours.
- Recurring vendor payments under an existing signed contract do not require re-approval.

## Purchase Order Process

1. Requestor submits PO request with cost center and business justification.
2. System routes to the correct approver based on the table above.
3. Approved POs are sent to the vendor by Procurement, not by the requesting team directly.
4. Any PO tied to a new (non-approved) vendor requires a Vendor Risk review before submission — allow 5 business days.

## Common Bottleneck

The most common delay is missing cost-center codes on the initial request, which bounces the PO back to the requestor instead of routing forward. Always confirm the cost center before submission.
